“Just because another door opens doesn’t mean you need to run through it yelling ‘destiny!’”

Opportunities are great — until there are too many of them. Then they start feeling less like freedom and more like a carnival of FOMO. Every new option whispers, “Maybe this is the real path!” while your original plan quietly waves from the corner like, “Hey… remember me?” Staying the course isn’t stubbornness; it’s focus with boundaries. Every shiny alternative costs time, energy, and half a chapter of your sanity. The point isn’t to take every road — it’s to finish the one you’re actually on. So today, when temptation to pivot strikes, pause. Ask if it’s growth or just boredom wearing a new outfit. The horizon only gets clearer when you stop changing maps mid-journey.
