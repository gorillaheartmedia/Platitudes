October 23

“Being caught up is nice. Brief. Mysterious. Like spotting a rainbow over a clean inbox.”

For a fleeting moment, everything’s done — dishes washed, emails answered, brain unclenched. You almost don’t know what to do with yourself. Then, of course, something new pops up, and the universe chuckles softly: “Nice try.” Being caught up isn’t a permanent state; it’s a snapshot between cycles. The goal isn’t to live there — it’s to visit often enough to remember what breathing feels like. The real peace comes from accepting that “done for now” is still done. So today, if you find yourself momentarily ahead, enjoy it. Don’t go looking for the next emergency. Just let the pause be the reward before life inevitably restocks your list.
