May 31

"Adaptability"

Adaptability isn’t about giving up control — it’s about learning to dance with what shows up. It’s the grace between expectation and surprise, the calm nod when plans unravel, and the quiet skill of turning “not this” into “something else.” It’s what lets the traveler find joy in the delay, the artist find beauty in the mistake, the tired soul find rest in the unplanned pause. Adaptability doesn’t resist change — it reintroduces itself to it. So as May closes, loosen your grip on certainty. Let life move as it does — sideways, backward, beautifully out of order. You don’t have to predict the rhythm; you just have to keep your balance.
