April 15

“The staircase never promises how many steps there are.”

You start climbing, confident — then halfway up, your legs stage a rebellion and you question every life choice that led you here. Still, you keep going, because stairs don’t argue or explain. They just rise. Staircases remind us that progress looks repetitive until it doesn’t. Each step feels the same — small, tiring, ordinary — until suddenly you’re somewhere new. Of course, they also remind us that pride and gravity are mortal enemies. So today, take the next step. Don’t count them all — just climb. You’ll know you’ve arrived when the view changes… or when you’re too out of breath to care.
