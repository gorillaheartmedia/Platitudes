December 1

“Reflection: the annual highlight reel of things that made sense eventually.”

December arrives with a clipboard and a raised eyebrow, asking how the year went. You try to answer honestly, but where do you start? The chaos? The surprises? The days that felt like twelve in one? Reflection isn’t about tidy summaries — it’s about noticing that you’re still here, still learning, still becoming. Looking back doesn’t mean reliving every moment; it means recognizing the through line — the quiet persistence that carried you through all twelve acts of this particular comedy-drama. You adapted, stumbled, grew, and occasionally nailed it on the first try. That counts. So today, take a small victory lap in your mind. You’ve been busy being human — brilliantly, imperfectly, continuously. The year might not have gone as planned, but you went through it — and that’s worth remembering kindly.
