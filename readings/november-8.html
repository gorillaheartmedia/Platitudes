November 8

“The universe has impeccable comic timing — unfortunately, we’re usually the punchline.”

You ever notice how life drops its surprises with perfect dramatic flair? You spill coffee right before the meeting, bump into an ex right after you say you’re over them, or get life advice from someone who clearly shouldn’t be giving any. That’s comic timing — the universe’s way of reminding us we’re in a story, not a spreadsheet. The trick isn’t avoiding the awkward beats; it’s learning to laugh on cue. Humor doesn’t erase chaos — it just gives it rhythm. So today, if everything feels absurdly timed, lean into it. You’re not failing — you’re just part of a very elaborate cosmic joke. And sometimes, laughing with it is the closest thing to wisdom we get.
