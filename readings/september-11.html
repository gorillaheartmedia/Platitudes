“Belonging: the art of being accepted for your weirdness, not despite it.”

We all want to find our people — the ones who laugh at the same nonsense, forgive your typos in real time, and don’t flinch when you say, “Okay, this might sound insane but…” Belonging isn’t about blending in; it’s about relaxing enough to stop performing. The right people don’t make you earn your place — they just hand you a metaphorical chair and say, “Scoot in.” So today, don’t chase popularity; look for resonance. You don’t have to be everyone’s flavor — just someone’s favorite kind of strange.
