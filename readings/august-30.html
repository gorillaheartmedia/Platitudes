“Trust the process — yes, even this weird part.”

Everyone says it like it’s easy, but “the process” never looks trustworthy while you’re in it. It’s messy, confusing, and keeps losing your paperwork. Progress feels suspiciously like chaos wearing business casual. But here’s the thing: doubt doesn’t mean you’re off track — it means you’re awake for it. The process rarely announces that it’s working; it just quietly evolves while you’re busy complaining. So today, keep going — even if you’re side-eyeing every step. Growth doesn’t need your approval; it just needs your attendance. Besides, the process has a great track record once it’s over.
