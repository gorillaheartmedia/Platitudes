December 24

“Christmas Eve: the calm before the wrapping paper storm.”

There’s a special kind of stillness on Christmas Eve — that last deep breath before the paper flies and the batteries go missing. The world seems to hold its breath, glowing softly under too many lights, while everyone quietly hopes they remembered all the gifts. It’s the one night when time feels both endless and brief. Kids whisper in doorways, adults negotiate oven space, and somewhere a single bulb refuses to light — a humble reminder that perfection isn’t required for magic. So tonight, pause for a moment in the glow — between the noise, before the morning. Let the warmth of it all soak in: the people, the laughter, the fact that you made it here again. Tomorrow will be chaos — wonderful, noisy chaos — but for now, this is peace.
