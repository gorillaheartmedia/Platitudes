“Recovery isn’t starting over — it’s remembering where you were headed.”

We talk about getting “off track” like it’s a moral failure, but honestly, detours are just part of navigation. Everyone veers. The trick is not panicking and setting up permanent residence in the ditch. Recovering your course doesn’t require drama — just direction. You don’t have to fix the past or erase the wobble; you just have to face forward again. Progress rarely travels in straight lines — it zigzags, pauses for snacks, and occasionally takes the wrong exit. So today, skip the guilt and grab the wheel. Real growth isn’t staying perfectly aligned — it’s getting good at gentle corrections. After all, you’re still moving, and that’s what counts.
