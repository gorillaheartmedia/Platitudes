May 5

“The fiesta doesn’t wait for perfection — it starts when the music does.”

Cinco de Mayo isn’t about flawless plans or polished moves. It’s about rhythm, laughter, and maybe forgetting how salsa got on the ceiling. It’s joy that shows up uninvited and refuses to leave until the chips are gone. Celebrations like this remind us that life doesn’t need a reason to be loud — just a moment that feels too alive to stay quiet. Of course, they also remind us that tomorrow’s regret is usually spelled S.A.L.S.A. So today, raise a taco, dance badly, and let joy make a glorious mess of your schedule. Perfection can wait — the party can’t.
