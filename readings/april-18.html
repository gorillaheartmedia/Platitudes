April 18

“The typewriter never needed a software update.”

It clacked, it smacked, it made noise that sounded like progress. No distractions, no pop-ups, no autocorrect arguing with your genius. Just ink, paper, and the satisfying ding of completion. Simple, honest, and merciless — because mistakes weren’t deletable, only livable. Typewriters remind us that creativity once came with commitment. Each word hit the page like it meant it. Of course, they also weighed forty pounds and jammed whenever you felt inspired. So today, write like a typewriter: loud, deliberate, imperfect. Don’t backspace your thoughts — let them land, flaws and all. There’s poetry in the clatter.
