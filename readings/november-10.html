“Inconvenience: the universe’s favorite delivery system for perspective.”

You spill your drink, miss the bus, or discover that your “easy” DIY project now requires a small engineering degree. It’s easy to grumble — but sometimes the most inconvenient moments end up being the ones that actually wake you up. Inconvenience reminds you you’re alive — sometimes aggressively. It teaches flexibility, humility, and the subtle art of muttering politely at inanimate objects. More importantly, it invites curiosity: What if this detour isn’t a disaster, just a different route? So today, when life trips you up, resist the reflex to curse the delay. Smile (or grimace artistically). The road’s still leading somewhere — and the potholes are just there for character development.
