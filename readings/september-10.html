“Community: because doing life solo has terrible customer support.”

We like to think we’re independent, but let’s be honest — someone else probably reminded you to drink water today. Community isn’t about grand gestures or perfect harmony; it’s about the quiet web of people who keep you from Googling “how to human.” We don’t always pick our tribe — sometimes it’s just whoever shows up with jumper cables, snacks, or unsolicited advice that accidentally works. And that’s enough. So today, show up for someone — even awkwardly. Text first, listen more, lend the metaphorical cup of sugar. The magic of community isn’t in agreement; it’s in the shared chaos of trying to figure things out together.
