“Overthinking: because why experience one problem when you can experience it thirty-seven different ways?”

Your brain means well. It just assumes every scenario needs a director’s cut, a sequel, and a behind-the-scenes interview. Before you know it, you’ve solved, re-solved, and emotionally aged three years over something that hasn’t even happened. Overthinking feels productive, but it’s really just anxiety doing cosplay as logic. The plot rarely improves with more revisions. So today, let a few thoughts expire without feedback. Don’t dissect every text, tone, or cosmic signal. Sometimes the best move isn’t thinking it through — it’s thinking it less.
