April 22

“The bus stop is faith disguised as public seating.”

You stand there, ticket in hand, pretending not to check the schedule for the fifteenth time. The world rushes by, and you wait — not knowing if the bus will be late, early, or simply mythical. Still, you stay. Because waiting is believing. Bus stops remind us that movement often begins in stillness. You can’t control the timing, but you can show up, ready. Of course, they also remind us that weather has impeccable comedic timing — rain loves punctuality more than buses do. So today, trust the pause. You’re not stuck — you’re stationed. The next ride always comes, even if it takes the scenic route.
