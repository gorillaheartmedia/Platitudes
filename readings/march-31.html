March 31

“Grace isn’t about perfection"

it’s about movement without panic, strength without noise. It’s the pause before the stumble, the smile after the spill, the quiet decision to keep your dignity even when life feels like a blooper reel. It lives in small gestures: holding the door, forgiving yourself for the burnt toast, saying “thank you” when you’d rather say “really?” Grace doesn’t fix the world — it softens it, makes room for better timing, gentler choices, and the occasional second chance. So as March closes, move with grace. Let mistakes breathe, let joy land softly, and let the world spin without demanding applause. April can handle the rush — March ends beautifully when you don’t.
