“Your brain is not a storage unit.”

Some days, your mind feels like a messy attic — full of half-finished thoughts, emotional souvenirs, and the haunting memory of something you forgot to do in 2017. No wonder you’re tired. You’ve been tripping over mental furniture that doesn’t even belong to you anymore. Decluttering the mind isn’t about erasing everything — it’s about sorting through the noise. Keep what serves you, donate what doesn’t. You don’t need to hold onto every mistake, conversation, or what-if. Let them go. Space is a form of clarity. So today, tidy up inside. One thought at a time. Sweep out the “shoulds,” dust off the small joys, and maybe open a window of perspective. A little mental room goes a long way — and it’s much easier to find peace when it’s not buried under old worries.
