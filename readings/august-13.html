August 13

“Life doesn’t follow the script because it never got the memo.”

We plan, prepare, rehearse — and then life ad-libs the whole scene. You can’t choreograph the chaos; you can only learn to dance without tripping over your expectations. Unpredictability isn’t sabotage — it’s the universe’s way of keeping the story interesting. If everything went as planned, you’d be bored by Tuesday and narrating your grocery list for excitement. So today, stop demanding plot control. Improvise. Miss your train, lose your place, laugh at the absurdity. Life’s rewriting itself in real time — you might as well help punch up the dialogue.
