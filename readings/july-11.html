“Self-respect is the quiet cousin of confidence.”

Confidence makes noise; self-respect just stands there, steady and unbothered. It doesn’t need applause or proof — it knows its own weight. You can’t fake it, and you can’t buy it with productivity points or other people’s approval. Self-respect grows in small choices: leaving when you’re dismissed, speaking when it matters, resting when you’re tired instead of waiting for permission. It’s less about pride, more about alignment — a quiet yes to your own worth. So today, skip the pep talk. Just act like someone you already trust. The rest of you will catch up to the example.
