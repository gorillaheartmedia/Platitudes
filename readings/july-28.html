“Limitations are just creativity’s personal trainers.”

We hate constraints — too little time, too few options, not enough coffee — but without them, we’d never have to get inventive. Abundance makes us lazy; restriction makes us clever. Resourcefulness isn’t about having everything — it’s about realizing you already do, just not in the configuration you expected. The magic isn’t in more supplies; it’s in better rearranging. So today, let your limits shape the work instead of stall it. Great art, good decisions, and decent sandwiches have all been born from running out of something.
