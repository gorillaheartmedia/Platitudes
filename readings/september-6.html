September 6

“Change never sends a calendar invite.”

It just shows up — unannounced, underdressed, and apparently staying for dinner. One day everything fits, and the next you’re emotionally allergic to your own routine. We act surprised every time, as if life was ever supposed to stay the same. Change isn’t personal; it’s just evolution’s way of checking if you’re still paying attention. So today, don’t resist the remix. Let things rearrange, even if it feels weird. Progress rarely knocks politely — it just moves your furniture and dares you to adapt.
