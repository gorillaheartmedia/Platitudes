November 30

“Gratitude: the quiet encore at the end of the month.”

After all the meals, the chaos, the laughter, and the questionable leftovers, November ends not with fanfare, but with a full heart. Gratitude isn’t loud — it’s the soft hum that lingers when the day winds down and you finally notice the good that made it through. It’s in the people who stuck around, the routines that held steady, the surprises that didn’t ruin everything after all. Gratitude doesn’t demand perfection — just awareness. The more you notice, the more there is to notice. So tonight, take a small bow. You made it through another month of being human — messy, marvelous, still trying. Say thanks for what stayed, what changed, and what’s still finding its way. Gratitude, it turns out, is how we keep the lights on between seasons.
