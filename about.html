About Us

Welcome to Platitudes

We are the only team brave enough to admit that we have no idea what we’re doing — and somehow made that our brand. Founded accidentally during a meeting that was supposed to be about something else, Platitudes began with a simple mission:

“To say things that sound deep… until someone actually thinks about them.”

Our Team

  • Visionaries who still haven’t located the vision.
  • Researchers who exclusively cite fortune cookies.
  • Writers who believe deadlines are a social construct.
  • And one intern who insists on calling everything “content.”

Our site was built to look profressional but was created during our breaks so the bosses thought we looked busy. Procrastination at is entry level finest

Our Core Values

  • Ambiguity: If it’s clear, it’s wrong.
  • Overconfidence: We’re 100% sure about 60% of this.
  • Iterative Brilliance: Version 1 was bad. Version 2 will be worse, but ironically.
  • Community: Because misery loves company.

Our Motto

“It’s not about the destination. Or the journey. It’s about the snacks along the way.”
